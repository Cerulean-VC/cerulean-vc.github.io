## The scarce asset is proprietary context

AI is making generic software and knowledge production abundant. The scarce asset is shifting to physical-world data, operational workflows, verification rights, and domain judgment that cannot be scraped from the internet or reproduced by an API wrapper. We invest where that scarcity is highest: food and agriculture, energy systems, sustainable supply chains, industrial manufacturing, and oceans.

## Every durable company becomes a token factory

It supplies, owns, or orchestrates the data, identity, money, claims, measurements, and expertise that feed the agentic economy. The most valuable token streams are physical, private, asset-linked, messy, and economically consequential. The winning loop: win a high-friction workflow, earn privileged physical-world data, convert it into representations and trusted decisions, then turn uncertainty into priced risk.

## Capital-light, physics-constrained

We do not fund $100M pre-training runs, generic AI wrappers, or products that rely on green premiums. We back small, deterministic, physics-aware models and the companies that own the workflows where their outputs change real decisions — interconnection, procurement, underwriting, verification, routing, capital allocation.

## Open the shared layers, own the operating loops

The physical economy needs open commons — maps, identifiers, evals, methodologies — and companies that own workflow access, private data rights, customer trust, and deployment loops on top. We underwrite the boundary between the two.
