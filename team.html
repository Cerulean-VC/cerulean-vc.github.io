How we work

Cerulean is a two-GP firm with no platform team — we built one instead. Scout, our in-house agentic operating system, runs on the firm's own context: it prepares our meetings, drafts our letters, watches our pipeline, and learns from every correction. Partner-led and taste-driven, with machine leverage where it compounds. We invest in founders making the physical world legible to machines because we run our own firm the same way.
