AI will finish what software started.

Software made the information economy machine-readable. Media, finance and commerce moved onto computers because their work was already information.

The physical economy stayed behind. Farms, grids, factories, supply chains and oceans run on data that is messy, fragmented, regulated, local and full of tacit expertise. Software could record their transactions. It could not read their operations.

AI can. Models turn satellite imagery, grid telemetry and field records into maps, forecasts and decisions inside the systems operators already use.
